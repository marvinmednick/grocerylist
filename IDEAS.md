# Ideas

AI-generated enhancement suggestions that haven't been reviewed or prioritized. These are **not committed work items** — they're a holding area for concepts that might be worth exploring.

**How items get here:** During `/spec`, Claude separates genuinely deferred scope from its own suggestions. User triages each suggestion as: backlog (tracked), ideas (shelved here), or discard.

**When to review:** During `/design` (scan for ideas relevant to the feature being designed) and `/complete` (scan for ideas relevant to what was just shipped). Discard anything superseded by recent work.

**Groups:** Organize by topic area, not by when generated. Keep entries to one line each.
